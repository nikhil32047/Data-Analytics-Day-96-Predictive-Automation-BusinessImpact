Expert Data Analytics Lessons

🔹 Lesson 1: Predictive Analytics Fundamentals
=================================================
Summary:
Predictive analytics uses historical data + statistical models to forecast future outcomes.
Common models: Linear Regression, Decision Trees, ARIMA
Steps: Collect → Clean → Model → Validate → Predict

Example:
A telecom company predicts which customers are likely to churn based on call patterns and complaints.

🔹 Lesson 2: Automating Data Workflows
============================================
Summary:
Automation saves hours by scheduling routine data cleaning, loading, and reporting.
Tools: Airflow, Power Automate, Python scripts
Cron jobs or cloud-based automation (e.g., AWS Lambda)

Example:
A retail analyst schedules a daily ETL job at midnight → loads new sales data into dashboards automatically.

Video: Automating Data Pipelines with Python – 12 mins


🔹 Lesson 3: Model Evaluation & Validation
=============================================
Summary:
Before trusting a model, you must evaluate it.
Metrics: R², RMSE (for regression), Accuracy, Precision, Recall (for classification)
Cross-validation ensures stability

Example:
Your sales prediction model shows R² = 0.92 → explains 92% of variance → excellent fit!

Video: Model Evaluation Metrics Simplified – 14 mins


🔹 Lesson 4: Business Impact Analysis
========================================
Summary:
Data is useless without action. Translate findings into business insights.
Measure ROI, cost savings, or growth impact
Create visuals showing before vs after implementing insights

Example:
After a pricing model update, profit margins rose by 8% in Q3 — clearly linked to data-driven decisions.

======================================================
Summary:
---------
Collect 12 months of sales & marketing spend
